# StayMate Grand - Guest Information

## Check-in and check-out
Standard check-in starts at 2:00 PM. Standard check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out depend on room availability and may have an additional charge.

## Breakfast
Breakfast is served every day from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM at The Courtyard restaurant on the ground floor. A vegetarian section is available every day.

## Wi-Fi
Complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel. Guests can connect to `StayMate-Guest` and authenticate using their room number and surname.

## Swimming pool
The rooftop swimming pool is open from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult.

## Gym
The fitness centre on level 2 is open 24 hours for resident guests. Room key access is required after 10:00 PM.

## Cancellation policy
Flexible bookings may be cancelled without charge until 6:00 PM, 24 hours before check-in. Late cancellations and no-shows may be charged one night's room rate. Promotional and non-refundable rates may follow different terms shown at booking.

## Housekeeping
Daily housekeeping is offered between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. Guests may request extra towels, pillows, toiletries, water bottles, or an additional cleaning through the front desk.

## Room service
Room service is available from 6:30 AM to 11:30 PM. Late-night options are available from a limited menu after 10:30 PM.

## Parking
Complimentary self-parking is available for resident guests, subject to availability. Valet parking is available at the main entrance.

## Airport transfer
Airport transfers can be arranged through the front desk at least four hours in advance. Charges depend on vehicle type and pickup time.
