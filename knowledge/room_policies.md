# StayMate Grand — Room Policies

## Check-in
Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM. Guests arriving earlier may leave luggage with the front desk free of charge. Early check-in is subject to room readiness and cannot be guaranteed unless a paid early-arrival arrangement has been confirmed.

## Check-out
Standard check-out is by 11:00 AM. Express check-out is available when payment and billing details are already settled. Late check-out is subject to availability and may carry a charge based on the departure time.

## Late check-out charges
Checkout between 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM may be charged at 25% of the nightly room rate. Between 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM, the charge may be 50%. After 6:00 PM, a full additional night may be charged.

## Identification
Guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in. International guests may be asked for a passport and visa details as required by local regulations. The name should match the primary guest on the reservation.

## Occupancy
Standard rooms are intended for up to two adults. Deluxe rooms may accommodate two adults and one child. Suites may accommodate up to three adults, or two adults and two children, depending on the bedding arrangement.

## Visitors
Visitors are welcome in public areas. Visitors entering guest-room floors may need to provide identification at the front desk. Overnight visitors must be added to the reservation before staying in the room.

## Smoking
StayMate Grand is a non-smoking hotel. Smoking is not allowed inside guest rooms, corridors, elevators, restaurants, or indoor public areas. A cleaning and deodorizing fee may apply when smoking occurs inside a room.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours are from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Guests should keep music, television, and conversations at a reasonable volume. Repeated disturbances may result in a warning from hotel staff.

## Room keys
Guests should keep keys and key cards secure. Lost keys or damaged cards must be reported to the front desk. Replacement access may require identity verification for security reasons.

## In-room valuables
A digital safe is provided in each guest room for passports, cash, jewelry, and other small valuables. Guests should avoid leaving valuable belongings unattended in public areas.

## Damage
Guests are responsible for deliberate or negligent damage to hotel property during their stay. Normal wear and tear is not treated as guest damage. Accidental damage should be reported promptly so staff can help.

## Pets
Pets are not permitted in standard guest rooms. Service animals assisting guests with disabilities are permitted in accordance with applicable requirements. Guests travelling with an animal should notify the hotel before arrival.

## Prohibited items
Open flames, portable cooking appliances, hazardous materials, and illegal substances are not permitted in guest rooms. Guests should contact the front desk before bringing unusually high-power electrical equipment.

## Room changes
Room changes are subject to availability. A guest moving to a higher room category may need to pay the rate difference. If a verified maintenance issue requires relocation, the hotel will try to provide a suitable alternative.
