# The Courtyard - Sample Menu

Breakfast buffet: ₹750 per guest.

Popular vegetarian dishes include paneer tikka, dal tadka, vegetable biryani, masala dosa, and seasonal vegetable pasta.

Popular non-vegetarian dishes include butter chicken, grilled fish, chicken biryani, and club sandwiches.

The all-day menu is served from 11:00 AM to 10:30 PM. Guests with food allergies should inform restaurant staff before ordering.
