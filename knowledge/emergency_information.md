# StayMate Grand — Emergency and Safety Information

## Emergency assistance
Guests facing an immediate threat to life or safety should contact local emergency services and inform the hotel front desk as soon as possible.

## Fire alarm
If the fire alarm sounds, guests should remain calm, leave the room if safe, and follow illuminated exit signs and staff instructions. Elevators should not be used during a fire evacuation.

## Fire in a guest room
If smoke or fire is discovered, the guest should leave the area, close the door if possible, notify the hotel, and move toward a safe exit.

## Evacuation
Evacuation maps are posted in or near guest rooms. Guests should use stairwells, avoid collecting unnecessary belongings, and proceed to the location directed by hotel staff.

## Medical emergency
For a serious medical emergency, guests should contact emergency medical services or ask the front desk to do so immediately. The hotel can assist with coordination but does not replace professional care.

## Minor illness
For non-emergency health concerns, the front desk can provide directions to nearby clinics or pharmacies. Staff should not recommend prescription drugs or provide a diagnosis.

## Security concern
Suspicious behavior, lost keys, unauthorized access attempts, or other security concerns should be reported to the front desk immediately. Guests should not confront suspicious individuals themselves.

## Lost room key
A lost room key or key card should be reported promptly. Staff may ask the guest to verify identity before issuing replacement access.

## In-room safe
Guests should store passports, cash, jewelry, and small valuables in the in-room safe when possible. A malfunctioning safe should be reported instead of forced open.

## Power outage
During a power interruption, emergency lighting and essential systems may remain available through backup power. Guests should avoid elevators if staff advise that power conditions are unstable.

## Severe weather
During severe rain, storms, extreme heat, or other hazardous weather, guests should follow hotel and local-authority guidance. Outdoor facilities may close temporarily.

## Earthquake
If an earthquake occurs, guests indoors should move away from windows and protect themselves from falling objects. After shaking stops, they should follow staff instructions.

## Flooding
Guests noticing significant water leakage or flooding should avoid electrical equipment near the affected area and report the issue immediately.

## Child safety
Children should be supervised in elevators, stairways, pool areas, balconies, and other potentially hazardous locations. A missing child should be reported immediately.

## Reporting an emergency
When reporting an emergency, guests should clearly state the room or location, describe the problem, and say whether anyone is injured or requires immediate assistance.
