# StayMate Grand — Room Service Guide

## Service hours
Room service is available daily from 6:30 AM to 11:00 PM. The final full-menu order should normally be placed by 10:30 PM.

## Late-night service
After 11:00 PM, the front desk may be able to arrange a limited selection of packaged snacks and beverages depending on stock and staffing.

## Ordering
Guests may place an order through the room telephone or the hotel assistant when the requested item is available. The order should include room number, items, quantities, and any dietary requirements.

## Delivery time
Most standard room-service orders are delivered within approximately 30 to 45 minutes. Delivery may take longer during peak meal periods, events, severe weather, or unusually high occupancy.

## Breakfast
Room-service breakfast is available from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM. Selected Indian, continental, and healthy breakfast options are available.

## Included breakfast
A room package that includes breakfast does not automatically include room-service delivery unless the package specifically states that room-service breakfast is included.

## Vegetarian options
A wide range of vegetarian dishes is available. The kitchen also handles non-vegetarian food, so guests with strict requirements should clearly explain them when ordering.

## Vegan options
Selected dishes can be adapted for vegan guests, including fruit, salads, rice dishes, vegetable preparations, and some breakfast items. Guests should ask staff to confirm ingredients.

## Food allergies
Guests with food allergies must mention the allergy before ordering. The kitchen handles common allergens such as dairy, wheat, nuts, soy, and eggs, so absolute allergen-free preparation cannot be guaranteed.

## Spice level
Many Indian dishes can be prepared mild, medium, or spicy. Some gravies and pre-prepared components already contain spice and cannot always be made completely non-spicy.

## Charges
Room-service prices may include or be subject to applicable taxes and service charges as shown on the menu or bill. Guests should review the final amount before confirming the room charge.

## Payment
Room-service charges can normally be posted to an active guest room. Direct payment may be requested when room-charge privileges are not enabled.

## Order changes
Guests should contact room service immediately if they need to change or cancel an order. Once food preparation has started, some items may still be chargeable.

## Tray collection
Guests may call housekeeping or room service for collection of used trays and dishes. Trays should not be left in corridors for long periods without notifying staff.
