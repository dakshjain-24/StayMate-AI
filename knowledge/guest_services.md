# StayMate Grand — Guest Services

## Front desk
The front desk operates 24 hours a day for registered guests. Staff can assist with check-in, check-out, room access, billing questions, luggage, wake-up calls, and hotel services.

## Wake-up calls
Guests may request a wake-up call for a specific time. A wake-up call is a convenience service and should not be the only alarm used for critical travel plans.

## Printing and scanning
Basic printing and scanning are available through the front desk or business centre. Large, color, or unusually complex print jobs may carry a fee.

## Parcel assistance
The front desk can receive parcels for registered or arriving guests when the guest name is clearly shown. Hazardous, illegal, oversized, perishable, or cash-on-delivery shipments may be refused.

## Pharmacy assistance
Hotel staff may provide directions to nearby pharmacies or assist with contacting an external pharmacy. Hotel employees do not prescribe medicines or provide medical diagnosis.

## Doctor assistance
If a guest requests medical help, the front desk can assist in contacting a local doctor, clinic, ambulance, or emergency service depending on the situation.

## Wheelchair
A limited number of wheelchairs may be available for temporary use within the property. Guests who require continuous mobility support should arrange their own equipment.

## Celebration arrangements
Simple birthday or anniversary arrangements may be requested in advance. Decorations, cakes, flowers, and special setups depend on availability and may carry additional charges.

## Flowers and gifts
The front desk may help arrange flowers, cakes, or small gifts through local suppliers. The final price depends on the selected item and supplier.

## Payments
The hotel accepts the payment methods displayed at the front desk and during booking. Foreign currency exchange is not guaranteed.

## Billing questions
Guests should review their bill before check-out and report disputed charges promptly. Charges may include accommodation, food, laundry, transport, spa services, taxes, or other authorized services.

## GST invoice
Guests requiring a GST invoice should provide accurate billing details before final settlement. Corrections requested after checkout may take additional time.

## Early breakfast
Guests leaving before normal breakfast hours may ask whether a packed breakfast can be arranged. Advance notice is required and availability depends on the meal package and kitchen.

## Feedback and complaints
Guests may report concerns to the front desk at any time. The hotel may create a housekeeping, maintenance, or management follow-up request when necessary.
