# StayMate Grand — Parking and Transportation

## Guest parking
Complimentary on-site parking is available for registered hotel guests, subject to space. Parking is not guaranteed for oversized vehicles.

## Valet assistance
Valet assistance may be available during busy arrival and departure periods. Guests should remove cash and valuables from the vehicle before handing over keys.

## Parking security
The parking area is monitored, but guests remain responsible for locking vehicles and securing belongings. Damage or security concerns should be reported promptly.

## Airport transfers
Paid airport transfers may be requested through the front desk. Guests should ideally request a transfer at least 6 hours in advance.

## Airport booking details
The hotel confirms the vehicle type, estimated price, pickup point, and pickup time before an airport transfer is considered final.

## Railway station transfers
Transfers to and from Varanasi Junction may be arranged subject to vehicle availability. Guests should provide the train number and expected arrival or departure time.

## Taxi assistance
The front desk can assist with arranging local taxis. Fares depend on destination, traffic, vehicle type, timing, and the external operator's rates.

## Auto-rickshaw guidance
Auto-rickshaws are commonly used for local transport in Varanasi. The hotel can guide guests to a suitable pickup point when needed.

## Old City access
Some narrow lanes and ghat-side areas cannot be reached directly by cars. Guests should expect to walk part of the route or use smaller local transport where permitted.

## Early departures
Guests with early flights or trains should arrange transport the night before whenever possible. The front desk can coordinate a wake-up call and luggage assistance.

## Driver waiting time
For pre-arranged transfers, waiting beyond the included period may create an additional charge. Guests facing delays should contact the hotel so the driver can be informed.

## Sightseeing vehicles
Half-day and full-day sightseeing cars may be arranged through local operators. Charges depend on itinerary, duration, vehicle category, parking, and waiting time.

## Scooter and motorcycle rentals
The hotel does not directly rent motorcycles or scooters. Staff may provide general information about nearby external rental operators.

## Accessible transport
Guests requiring a vehicle suitable for mobility equipment should request it in advance because suitable vehicles may be limited.
