# StayMate Grand — Cancellation and Refund Policy

## Standard refundable bookings
Unless a special rate states otherwise, a standard refundable booking may be cancelled without a room charge until 6:00 PM local hotel time on the day before check-in.

## Late cancellation
Cancellations after the free-cancellation deadline may be charged the first night's room rate plus applicable taxes. Remaining nights are released from the reservation once the cancellation is processed.

## Same-day cancellation
A cancellation made on the scheduled check-in date is considered a late cancellation unless the accepted booking terms specifically allow same-day cancellation.

## Non-refundable rates
Promotional or advance-purchase rates may be marked non-refundable. These reservations generally cannot be cancelled for a cash refund after payment has been captured.

## No-show
A guest may be treated as a no-show when they do not arrive by the end of the scheduled check-in date and have not contacted the hotel to retain the reservation. At least the first night may be charged.

## Early departure
Guests leaving before their booked checkout date should notify the front desk as soon as possible. Refunds for unused nights depend on the rate rules and the amount of advance notice provided.

## Booking modifications
Changing dates, room type, or guest details is subject to availability. Date changes may also change the nightly rate because prices can vary by day and demand.

## Stay extensions
A requested extension is confirmed only after the hotel verifies that the same room, or an acceptable replacement, is available for the additional nights. The total price is recalculated for the updated stay.

## Refund timing
When a refund is approved to the original payment method, the hotel normally begins processing within 2 business days. Banks and card issuers may take around 5 to 10 business days to display the credit.

## Duplicate reservations
Guests who accidentally create duplicate reservations should contact the hotel promptly with both booking IDs. Duplicate reservations are reviewed under the applicable rate rules and are not automatically refundable.

## Group reservations
Bookings of five or more rooms may be treated as group reservations and may have different deposit, cancellation, and modification rules. The written group confirmation takes priority over standard individual terms.

## Third-party reservations
Bookings made through an external travel website or agency may need to be modified or cancelled through that provider. The provider may control payment collection, deadlines, and refund processing.

## Cancellation confirmation
A cancellation is complete only after the booking status changes to cancelled or the guest receives a cancellation confirmation. Guests should retain the booking ID and cancellation confirmation until any refund is complete.

## Exceptional disruptions
During major travel disruptions or local emergencies, the hotel may introduce temporary flexibility. These cases are reviewed individually and may vary by booking source and rate type.
