# StayMate Grand — Housekeeping Services

## Daily cleaning
Standard room cleaning is provided once each day between approximately 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM. A missed cleaning can often be rescheduled later the same day depending on staff availability.

## Do Not Disturb
Housekeeping normally does not enter a room displaying a Do Not Disturb sign. If the sign remains for an unusually long time, management may attempt to contact the guest as part of a welfare check.

## Towels
Fresh towels are provided during routine room cleaning. Additional towels can be requested through the front desk, housekeeping, or StayMate.

## Bed linen
Bed linen is changed according to the hotel's cleaning cycle and after each guest departure. Guests may request an earlier change for a spill, medical need, or other reasonable requirement.

## Extra pillows
Additional pillows are available on request, subject to stock. Guests may request soft or firm pillows when the relevant type is available.

## Blankets
Extra blankets can be delivered by housekeeping. Delivery may take longer during late-night periods or periods of unusually high occupancy.

## Toiletries
Basic toiletries are replenished during housekeeping. Additional soap, shampoo, dental kits, shaving kits, or other standard supplies may be requested.

## Drinking water
Complimentary drinking water is placed in guest rooms according to hotel policy. Additional bottles may be requested, while premium beverages or large quantities may be chargeable.

## Preferred cleaning time
Guests may request a preferred cleaning time. The hotel will try to accommodate the request, but an exact time cannot always be guaranteed.

## Urgent cleaning
Spills, broken glass, or other urgent cleaning needs should be reported immediately so housekeeping can prioritize the request and reduce safety risks.

## Lost and found
Items found in rooms or public areas are transferred to the hotel's lost-and-found process. Guests should provide their name, stay dates, room number if known, and a clear description of the item.

## Lost-item storage
Most ordinary lost items are stored for up to 90 days. Perishable goods and low-value hygiene items may be disposed of earlier. Identification documents may follow additional security procedures.

## Laundry collection
Housekeeping can collect laundry bags from guest rooms. The guest should complete the laundry list and place garments inside the provided bag before collection.

## Maintenance found during cleaning
If housekeeping notices a leak, faulty light, damaged fixture, or similar problem, staff may create a maintenance request. The guest may be contacted if access is required while the room is occupied.
