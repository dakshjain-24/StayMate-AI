# StayMate Grand — Spa, Gym, and Pool

## Spa hours
The wellness spa is open daily from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. The last full treatment normally begins at 8:00 PM.

## Spa appointments
Guests may request an appointment through the front desk or spa reception. The requested time is not confirmed until therapist availability has been checked.

## Arrival for treatment
Guests should arrive around 10 minutes before a spa appointment to complete any required consultation and prepare for the treatment.

## Spa cancellation
Spa appointments cancelled at least 4 hours in advance normally do not incur a cancellation charge. A late cancellation or no-show may be charged up to 50% of the treatment price.

## Health information
Guests should tell spa staff about pregnancy, recent surgery, injuries, allergies, skin conditions, or other health concerns before treatment.

## Massage treatments
Typical treatments may include relaxation massage, deep-tissue massage, head and shoulder massage, and foot massage. Exact treatments and prices can vary.

## Gym hours
The fitness centre is open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Registered guests may use the gym without an additional entry fee.

## Gym equipment
The gym includes treadmills, stationary cycles, free weights, and basic strength-training equipment. Equipment should be used according to posted instructions.

## Gym age policy
Guests aged 16 or older may use the gym independently. Younger guests must be accompanied by an adult.

## Pool hours
The outdoor pool is open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, subject to weather, cleaning, and maintenance conditions.

## Pool safety
Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult. Running, diving in restricted areas, and bringing glass containers near the pool are not permitted.

## Pool towels
Pool towels are available at the pool desk during operating hours and should be returned after use.

## Swimwear
Appropriate swimwear is required in the pool. Clothing that may release dye, fibers, or other material into the water may not be permitted.

## Outside guests
Spa services may be available to non-resident guests with confirmed appointments. Gym and pool access is primarily for registered hotel guests unless a specific day-use arrangement has been approved.
