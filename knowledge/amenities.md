# StayMate Grand — Amenities and Facilities

## Wi-Fi
Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in guest rooms, the lobby, restaurants, and most public areas. Guests can connect to the StayMate Guest network using the access details provided at check-in.

## Wi-Fi troubleshooting
If a device cannot connect, guests should forget the network and reconnect before contacting the front desk. Persistent connectivity problems can be escalated to technical support.

## Swimming pool
The outdoor pool is open daily from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, subject to weather and maintenance. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult, and glass containers are not allowed.

## Fitness centre
The fitness centre is open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM and is complimentary for registered guests. It includes treadmills, exercise cycles, free weights, and basic strength equipment.

## Business centre
A small business centre near the lobby is open from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. It provides workstations, printing, scanning, and basic office support for registered guests.

## Meeting rooms
Meeting and event spaces can be arranged for corporate meetings, workshops, small conferences, and private gatherings. Capacity, catering, and audiovisual needs should be discussed with the events desk in advance.

## Luggage storage
Complimentary luggage storage is available before check-in and after check-out on the same day. Guests should not leave cash, jewelry, passports, medicines, or other valuables in stored luggage.

## Accessibility
Selected parts of the property support guests with mobility needs. Accessible room features are limited and should be requested before arrival so staff can confirm the appropriate room and route.

## In-room amenities
Guest rooms include air conditioning, television, an electric kettle, drinking water, basic toiletries, towels, a work surface, telephone, and a digital safe. Deluxe rooms and suites include additional seating.

## Laundry
Laundry and pressing services are available daily. Items collected before 9:00 AM are normally returned the same evening, while later collections may be returned the following day.

## Ironing
Irons and ironing boards are available on request. Guests should unplug the iron after use and allow it to cool before leaving it unattended.

## Baby equipment
A limited number of baby cots are available free of charge, subject to availability. High chairs are available in the main restaurant. Requests are best made before arrival.

## Power backup
Essential hotel services have backup power support. During a wider outage, some non-essential equipment may temporarily operate at reduced capacity.

## Elevators
Guest floors are served by elevators. During emergency conditions, guests should follow staff instructions and use stairwells when elevators are unavailable or unsafe.
